Pick an NPC

Each node is something the NPC says, with up to 5 replies. A reply can go to another node, run an action (wares, faction:<id>:<amount>, give:<item>, trade:blacksmith|outfitter|artificer, quest:<quest key>, end), and can be hidden until the player has a flag (a parley reward). A reply with neither ends the conversation.

Pick a merchant list

Prices are in copper (100 copper = 1 silver). A merchant NPC sells one list: set it on the Mobs tab (Merchant list).

Alternate advancement (aa.json)

List: general, archetype, class (with classes) or special. Cost: points per rank, one number or one per rank (comma separated). Stats: attribute bonus per rank ("constitution: 2"). Requires: other AA ids and the rank needed ("1001: 2"). Ids must be unique.

Housing plots (plots.json)

A plot for sale shows a For Sale sign; buying it lets the owner put up one of its buildings. Prefab is the compoundobjectMD.tab root prefab. Materials ("Item: n") make a building a construction project. NPCs are mob keys the building may hold.

Chains, counters and rescues

Chains (chains.json)

Counters (counters.json) and rescues (rescues.json): ability names per class, comma separated

A chain: landing the opener opens a reaction window (seconds) for the next step, and the last step for the finisher; steps and the finisher can only be used through the window. Counters are usable only while an enemy casts at the player; rescues when an NPC hits a groupmate. Ability names are display names ("Brutal Strike I"). Class names as in races_classes.

Riftway destinations

The key is the upper-case name the client sends for /rift. Color is the price tier on the map (green 50, blue 500, yellow 2000, red 5000 copper, purple: a hub, free here). Chunk, x and y are where travel lands; leave them empty for a destination that is not open on this server yet. Riftway stones are mobs with "riftway": key.

Pick a work order

A task master NPC hands out work orders. Accepting one gives the supplies; the player crafts the order's recipe (a recipe marked "work order only" on the Recipes tab) and hands in the deliveries for the reward. Abandoning takes the supplies back. Orders can be repeated. Difficulty is the skill level the order is moderate for.

Factions

Standing runs from -20000 to 20000. "Kill" is the change for killing one of its members (default -50). Allies change the same way when its members die, enemies the opposite way (ids, comma separated). A prestige faction stays hidden until the first change. Mobs join a faction on the Mobs tab; quests and parleys can reward standing.

Pick a recipe

A recipe turns its inputs into the output item at a crafting station. Each stage is a set of steps the player takes in order; a step costs action points (AP) out of the recipe's max AP, may need a tool in the bags and may use up a utility item. Complications happen during a stage and lower quality until their counter action is used. Action ids are the client's crafting actions (30001 and up here are placeholders).

Pick cards, the deck or a parley

Cards are the diplomacy strategies. Each has an expression, a push (bar points toward whoever plays it), a cost and a gain in expression points, and a refresh in rounds. The parley window shows no Demand pool, so cards should use Reason, Inspire or Flattery. A parley belongs to the NPCs whose Mobs tab "parley" names it. The bar runs from 0 to 21 and starts at 11.

Changes submitted by contributors wait here until an admin approves (saves) or rejects them.
